# Game Design Document

## Premise

Demons.
Aliens.
Cyborgs.
Mutants.
Ghouls.

They all fight to obtain a unique metal called **AUR**, short for *Advanced Ultraalloy*. In the entire universe, only **Ultratech** can produce it.

AUR is made from fresh human flesh combined with three supermetals found only in `Mare Crisium`, forged in a furnace hotter than hell.

## Why Does Everyone Want AUR?

AUR is the only known material capable of making faster-than-light travel possible.

It possesses extraordinary properties:

* It is **self-healing**.
* It never degrades.
* It is virtually indestructible.
* It is extremely toxic, even in tiny amounts.

## Setting

The year is **3000**.

After the `great collapse`, caused by *superintelligence*, and countless failed attempts to colonize Mars, humanity turned its attention to the Moon. There, it began searching for precious supermetals that could sustain further advances in artificial intelligence.

Following the disaster, connected and automated systems were banned or considered too dangerous. As a result, the factory relies on:

* Human operators
* Dummy & Old Isolated or analogic devices
* Manual bureaucratic processes

Ultratech already had bases scattered across the Moon. Some of them had been overrun by `unknown creatures`.

Eventually, Ultratech began luring humans into working at its factory in Mare Crisium.

That was when everything began.

The company also started growing humans in pods, intending to use them later as raw material for the production of AUR.

## Ultratech

Ultratech was one of the first companies to reach a valuation of **$1 trillion**, driven by its promise to colonize worlds beyond Earth.

During one of its missions to the Moon, the company discovered several supermetals beneath Mare Crisium. Ultratech then launched multiple research programs designed to profit from the discovery.

These experiments eventually led to the creation of AUR.

## The Mare Crisium Incident

Ultratech discovers the three materials beneath Mare Crisium.

The following events occur:

1. The first batch of AUR is successfully produced.
2. The first spatial-distortion test tears open something beyond physical space.
3. Aliens detect the resulting emission and arrive on the Moon.
4. Demons cross through the rifts opened by the material.
5. Workers exposed to AUR's toxicity become mutants.
6. Discarded bodies and defective batches give rise to ghouls.
7. Ultratech creates hybrid cyborgs to defend and operate the factory.

These events transform the Mare Crisium factory into a battleground. Demons, aliens, cyborgs, mutants, and ghouls all fight to control AUR and the only facility capable of producing it.

## Producing AUR

AUR requires three supermetals found beneath Mare Crisium.

### `void-oscillating rhenium`

A black metal found deep beneath Mare Crisium.

Its structure oscillates between microscopic physical states. This property allows AUR to distort space, making it the key component required for faster-than-light travel.

### `hyperelastic exotic lattice`

A metal with a crystalline structure that spontaneously returns to its original configuration when deformed or broken.

It gives AUR its self-healing properties. In its pure state, broken fragments slowly attempt to rejoin one another.

### `neutron-exposed xenometal`

An extremely dense and radioactive metal formed by unknown processes beneath the lunar surface.

It makes AUR extraordinarily resistant and virtually indestructible.

### `fresh human flesh`

Fresh human flesh must be harvested on-site.

The three supermetals are incompatible with one another. When melted together, they destroy each other or collapse. Human organic matter acts as a matrix, allowing the three materials to coexist without annihilating one another.

## The Production Process

1. The three supermetals and the fresh human flesh are combined.
2. Every ingredient is frozen to *absolute zero*.
3. In a fraction of a second, the entire mixture is melted in a furnace heated to the temperature of the *Sun's core*.
4. The ingredients burn and mix together for **36 hours**.
5. At the end of the process, only ***666 grams*** of AUR are produced.

## Who You Are

You play as a *human* `identity compliance operator`.

You have never had any real memories. Every memory you possess was fabricated while you were growing inside a pod.

Your entire existence was carefully shaped to prepare you for your current role.

## Factions

The factions fighting for control of AUR are:

* Demons are magical beings that can read simple minds.
* Aliens resemble giant praying mantises with sharp blades.
* Cyborgs are half-human, half-machine, possessing immense strength.
* Mutants are former humans exposed to extreme toxicity. They are severely deformed.
* Ghouls have lost consciousness and are aggressive.
