# Intro

Demons
Aliens
Cyborgs
Mutants
Ghouls

They all fight to obtain a unique metal called Uru. In the entire universe, only Ultratech produces it.

Uru is made from human flesh combined with other supermetals in a forge hotter than hell.

Everyone wants Uru. You work at the only factory capable of producing it. To keep production running, you must decide who gets in, who becomes raw material, and who leaves with the metal.
